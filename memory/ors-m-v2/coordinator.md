## Planning requirements from confirmed experience

When you split this task into sub-graphs and choose their skills, follow each requirement whose condition holds for this task. Each one comes from failures of earlier tasks that were repaired and confirmed by a rerun.

1. When transferring an object onto a support or into a container, establish distinct source and destination geometry before grasping. Bind transport to completed grasping, and completion to completed placement. If either target is unresolved or a stage fails, re-perceive the affected targets and retry that stage before advancing.
2. When carrying a hollow object or attachment is uncertain, insert a possession check after a test lift and before transport. Require gripper-state evidence together with observed object motion consistent with being held. On failure, re-perceive and retry grasping with a bounded count; if exhausted, refresh geometry and select an alternative grasp before carrying.
3. When placing into a container, onto a support, or leaving a part closed, verify the requested relation from fresh observations after release and withdrawal, using the assigned destination or sub-region. For hollow-object transfers also check possession before transport. On failure, refresh geometry and retry the failed grasp, release, placement or closure; finish only when the relation holds.
